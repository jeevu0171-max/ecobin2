Smart Waste Management for Cleaner Cities

Real-Time Monitoring, Intelligent Collection, and Automated Municipal Alerts powered by IoT and GPS Navigation.

Urban Waste Problems

  • Overflowing Dustbins

    Public bins overflow, causing garbage spillages on walkways and streets before collection trucks arrive.

  • Delayed Waste Collection

    Fixed-schedule collections lead to fuel wastage for empty bins, while overflowing ones remain unaddressed.

  • Environmental Pollution

    Unattended toxic wastes attract rodents and pollute aquifers, degrading quality of life in cities.

  • Public Health Concerns

    Open composting triggers disease vectors, foul odor, and microbial spread in densely populated zones.

The EcoBin Smart Solution

  • IoT Smart Bins

    Bins continuously measure fill levels and report data to central clouds via wireless sensors.

  • GPS Tracking

    Real-time GPS tracking pairs filled bins with nearby municipal collectors automatically.

  • Automated Alerts

    Crossing the 80% threshold alerts nearest drivers via instant SMS coordinates and dashboard alerts.

  • Smart Route Optimization

    Shortest route paths are plotted dynamically to cut down on response windows and reduce fuel use.
